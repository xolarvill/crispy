# crispy — the marketing workbench

## Product identity

Brand name: **crispy** (lowercase). Fixed signature: **the marketing workbench**. Full lockup: **crispy — the marketing workbench**. Chinese category: 营销工作台.

crispy brings marketing research, creative work, publishing, performance and reusable knowledge into a shared workspace. It helps operators move from understanding a market and product to creating, reviewing, scheduling and learning from marketing work. Creative production is a module; pipelines and agents are execution mechanisms underneath the workspace.

## Product areas

- Product research: product, store, industry and audience context. Current backend research is store-scoped; SKU-specific research is not yet an independent workflow.
- Competitor research: competitive context and supporting evidence.
- Creative work: tasks, product references, image/video assets, review and delivery.
- Publishing: channel scheduling and publication workflows.
- Performance: shop/project metrics, integrations and feedback.
- Memory: reusable knowledge, evidence and operator governance.
- Connections: shops, channels and credential environment-variable mappings.

The frontend currently offers initial read views for research, memory, schedules and metrics. Full research execution, scheduling management and deeper analytics still require migration. Future local gateway/MCP and Magpie integration are direction, not shipped claims.

## Brand voice and interface

Use direct, concise operational language. Prefer “任务”, “素材”, “研究”, “发布”, “数据”, “记忆”; avoid describing the whole product as a production pipeline, generation factory or orchestration system. Technical pipeline terminology remains valid in internal contracts, APIs and diagnostics.

Keep forest green, warm neutral surfaces, the existing layered mark and a compact wordmark. Keep the signature in README and product/brand documentation; do not place it under the sidebar wordmark. No slogan blocks, redundant top bars, H1/H2 UI elements or decorative dashboards. Familiar actions use Lucide icons with accessible names. Marketing breadth does not justify extra chrome or speculative navigation.
